# D-014 — Original synthesized music and sound effects

Accepted under the owner's 2026-10-13 instruction (sound and music approved; "you have my
permission for all"). No purchase was made.

- Asset sites (Kenney, OpenGameArt, Freesound) are unreachable from the build environment, and
  buying a pack was never authorized, so every sound is generated by `tools/audio/synth.py`
  from sine and noise building blocks. There are no samples and no third-party material, so
  the files carry no licence obligations.
- Output: `game/assets/audio/*.ogg` — a 44 s F-major yard theme (piano, music box, pad,
  bass, soft shaker; rendered twice so the reverb tail wraps into a seamless loop), yard
  ambience (breeze and bird calls), and effects for buttons, toggles, care, coins, day end,
  birth, the race bell and canter hoofbeats.
- `GameAudio` (scene-tree-root node) keeps music playing across screens, clicks every button
  automatically and stores a mute setting in `user://settings.cfg` (♪ button on the title
  screen and the stable top bar).
- Regenerate with `python3 tools/audio/synth.py` (needs numpy and ffmpeg).
- Future: a licensed or commissioned score can replace these files with the same names.
